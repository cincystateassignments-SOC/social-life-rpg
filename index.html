HOW TO PLAY

Explore

Walk around Lakeview. A floating ! marks people or objects relevant to your current investigation.

Observe

Talk to people before deciding what a situation means. Sociology begins by looking beyond the first explanation.

Think sociologically

Your choices do not award points for opinions. They unlock concepts when you use sociological reasoning and evidence.

Save

Progress saves automatically in this browser using local storage.
